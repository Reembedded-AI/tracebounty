# Privacy

- Nothing is uploaded without your explicit, per-submission consent given in the `review` step, where the full payload is displayed.
- Trace-only: we do not capture your repository, environment, or files beyond what already appears in the session transcript.
- Your working directory path is never uploaded; only a SHA-256 fingerprint of it (for dedup).
- You can request deletion of any submission: email privacy@reembedded.dev with the submission id (shown by `tracebounty status`).
- Accepted submissions may be licensed to AI labs as evaluation and training data under our terms of service, which you accept at login. That is the whole point of the bounty; do not submit anything you cannot license.
